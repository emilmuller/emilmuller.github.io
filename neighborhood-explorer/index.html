Neighborhood Explorer

For Android and iPhone

Neighborhood Explorer helps you get to know the area around your home on foot. Record your walks, see which streets you have already covered, and plan routes that take you somewhere new.

There is no account, no ads and no analytics. Your walks and your home location stay on your phone.
